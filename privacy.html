Privacy Policy — Tube Play

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Tube Play ("the app") is an Android app that lets you watch YouTube on older Android phones. This policy explains what information the app accesses, and how it is used, stored and shared.

1. Information the app accesses

When you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests permission through Google Sign-In for:

  • Basic profile information: your name, email address and profile picture (Google scopes: openid, email, profile). This is used only to show that you are signed in and to display your account inside the app.
  • Your YouTube account, read-only: for example your subscriptions and liked videos (Google scope: youtube.readonly). This is used only to show your own subscriptions, history and library inside the app. The app never uploads, edits or deletes anything on your YouTube account.

Tube Play never asks for, sees or stores your Google password. Sign-in happens on Google's own sign-in screen.

2. How your information is used

Your Google account information is used only to sign you in and to display your own YouTube content in the app. It is not used for advertising, and it is not sold or shared with any third party.

3. Where your information is stored

Your account information and sign-in tokens are stored only on your own Android device, inside the app. Tube Play has no server of its own: no account data is sent to us or to any other server, except to Google's own services when you sign in or load YouTube content, as described above.

4. Videos and downloads

Videos are streamed from YouTube. If you download a video inside the app, the file is saved only in your own phone's Downloads folder. No viewing history is sent anywhere by the app itself.

5. Children

Tube Play is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Your choices

You can sign out of the app at any time, and you can remove Tube Play's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account settings (myaccount.google.com > Security > Third-party access). Signing out removes the account information stored on your device.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
