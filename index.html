▶ Tube Play

YouTube on your old Android phone — simple, fast, familiar.

What is Tube Play?

Tube Play is an Android app that lets you watch YouTube on older Android phones (Android 5.0 and above) where the official YouTube app no longer runs well. It looks and feels like the YouTube app you already know: Home, Shorts, Subscriptions, search, and your own account.

Features

  • Watch YouTube videos, Shorts and live streams on old phones.
  • Sign in with your Google account to see your subscriptions, history and library.
  • Search with your voice, change video quality, and keep playing in a small window while you browse.
  • Background audio keeps playing when you press Home or lock the screen.
  • Download a video you are watching for offline viewing on your own device.

How sign-in works

Tube Play uses Google Sign-In. When you sign in, Google asks for your permission to share your basic profile (name, email address and profile picture) and read-only access to your YouTube account (such as your subscriptions) so the app can show your own content. Tube Play never sees or stores your Google password. You can sign out or remove access at any time from your Google account settings.

Download

Tube Play is distributed as an Android app (APK) for personal use on your own phone. It is not the official YouTube app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Google LLC or YouTube.
